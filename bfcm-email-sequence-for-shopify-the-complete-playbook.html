BFCM Email Sequence for Shopify: The Complete Playbook

2026-07-14

Black Friday and Cyber Monday are the two biggest revenue days of the year for most Shopify stores, but only if you have an email sequence planned weeks in advance. A haphazard send-on-the-day approach leaves money on the table. A structured BFCM email sequence for Shopify stores converts subscriber segments differently, tests offers before launch day, and captures last-minute buyers with urgency.

This guide walks you through the exact sequence strategy, timing, and copywriting framework that generates 20-40% of annual revenue in a single week.

Why Your BFCM Email Sequence Matters More Than Paid Ads

Most Shopify store owners focus on paid advertising for Black Friday, then wonder why their email list sits quiet. Email is owned media. You don't pay per click. Your subscribers have already shown intent by signing up.

During BFCM week, email typically delivers:

  • Higher ROI than paid ads (email ROI averages 42:1 vs. 3-5:1 for paid)
  • Repeat purchases from warm audiences
  • Lower customer acquisition cost for year-round customers
  • A way to move inventory without destroying brand perception through aggressive discounts

The difference between a mediocre BFCM and a killer one is sequence structure. Random emails feel spammy. A sequence with clear segments, escalating urgency, and targeted offers feels like service to your customers.

The Optimal BFCM Email Sequence Timeline

Successful BFCM campaigns start planning in August and sending emails by October. Here's the realistic timeline:

Pre-BFCM Sequence (8-10 weeks before Black Friday)

Week 1-3: Tease and Build Anticipation

Send one teaser email announcing that BFCM deals are coming. Don't disclose the discount yet. Focus on what problem your product solves, not the price cut. Keep it light. Your goal is to warm up your list and get people expecting an offer.

Example subject line: "The best discount of the year is coming (sneak peek inside)"

Week 4-6: Segment Your Audience

Start tagging subscribers based on behavior:

  • Recent purchasers (bought in last 60 days)
  • Repeat customers (3+ purchases ever)
  • Engaged but never bought (opened emails, clicked links, never converted)
  • Inactive (haven't opened an email in 90 days)

This segmentation is critical. A repeat customer should get a different offer than a new subscriber. Inactive people might need a steeper discount.

Week 7-8: Run a Soft Launch

Send BFCM offers to your best segment (repeat customers) 1-2 weeks early. Offer 15-20% off to your VIP segment only. This does three things:

  1. Tests your email and checkout flow before the rush
  2. Creates FOMO when inactive subscribers see others talking about the deal
  3. Generates early revenue and captures stock-sensitive buyers

BFCM Week Sequence (The Heavy Hitting Emails)

Monday-Tuesday Before Black Friday: Launch Email

Send your main offer to the full list. This is your highest-stakes email. Subject line should include urgency and benefit.

Example: "Save 25% this Friday only (starts 12 PM ET)"

Keep the email short. Hero image at the top showing the offer. CTA button twice (top and bottom). Link directly to your sale collection, not a landing page (reduces friction).

Wednesday-Thursday: Scarcity and Urgency

Send a follow-up email highlighting limited stock or deadline. Only 30% of people open the first email. This catches the people who missed it.

Subject line: "Sale starts in 2 days: Only X units left at 25% off"

Friday: Flash Offer or Bundle

On Black Friday itself, send one email early (7-9 AM your customer's timezone). Offer a small flash discount (additional 10% off or free gift with purchase) for the next 12 hours.

Example: "Black Friday is here. Free gift on orders over $50 (today only)"

Saturday-Sunday: Cyber Monday Push

Don't stop emailing Friday. Cyber Monday converts well because weekend shoppers are relaxed and browsing. Send 1-2 emails with a fresh angle.

Saturday: "Still got time: Ends Sunday at midnight"

Sunday (afternoon): "Last 12 hours. 25% off ends tonight"

Tuesday After BFCM: Final Clearance (Optional)

Depending on your inventory position, send a last-chance email to cold segments (people who didn't open previous emails or made no purchase). This clears remaining stock and captures fence-sitters.

Segmentation Strategy That Drives Real Results

Your offer and messaging change by segment. Here's what works:

Repeat Customers

  • Offer: 20-25% off + free shipping
  • Angle: "VIP access" or "early sale for our best customers"
  • Timing: Send 2-3 days before main launch
  • Tone: Gratitude-first. "Thanks for shopping with us. Here's something special."

High-Engagement, Non-Buyers

  • Offer: 25-30% off (steeper discount to overcome purchase friction)
  • Angle: "Your first purchase" or "This is your sign to try"
  • Timing: Coordinate with main launch
  • Tone: Curiosity and benefit. "See why 5,000 people chose us this year."

Inactive Subscribers

  • Offer: 30-35% off (do not email them more than once; one strong push)
  • Angle: "We miss you"
  • Timing: Friday of BFCM week only
  • Tone: Low-pressure, genuine. "It's been a while. Here's a reason to come back."

New Subscribers

  • Offer: 20% off (in line with repeat customers; don't train them to expect 35% off)
  • Timing: Normal launch sequence
  • Tone: Welcome and product education. "Welcome to our community. Here's 20% off to start."

Copywriting Framework for Each Email

Every BFCM email should follow this structure:

Subject Line: Leads with benefit or urgency, not discount amount alone.

Examples that work: - "Save 25%, get it in time for holidays" - "Restock your favorites (25% off)" - "These don't come back: 25% off everything"

Hero Section: One clear image of your best-selling product or lifestyle shot showing the discount percentage or "SALE" badge prominently.

Body Copy: Two to three short paragraphs max.

  • Paragraph 1: State the offer and deadline (deadline is non-negotiable; it creates urgency).
  • Paragraph 2: Speak to a specific customer pain point. "Shop gifts for the hard-to-shop-for person in your life" or "Get your go-to piece before they sell out."
  • Paragraph 3 (Optional): Build social proof or scarcity. "5,000 orders already placed" or "Only 45 units left in our bestselling colorway."

Call-to-Action: Use action language. "Shop the Sale," "Get 25% Off," "Claim Early Access." The button should be a contrasting color (not gray).

Footer: Repeat your discount code if applicable. Include clear unsubscribe option (required by law and reduces complaints).

Testing and Timing Logistics

Send times matter. Test two send times with your biggest segment:

  • Test 1: Tuesday 10 AM ET
  • Test 2: Tuesday 6 PM ET

Send to 50% of the segment each. Open and click rates will tell you which time your audience prefers. Use that learning for future sends.

Also: Do not send more than two emails on the same day to the same segment. You will trigger unsubscribes and spam complaints.

Final Checklist Before You Hit Send

  • Segment your list into at least four groups (repeat customers, engaged non-buyers, inactive, new)
  • Schedule emails 6-8 weeks before Black Friday
  • Set clear deadlines in every email
  • Test one email to a small segment before sending full blast
  • Monitor complaint and unsubscribe rates (stay under 0.5% unsubscribe per send)
  • Have a backup email ready in case the first one underperforms
  • Confirm your discount code works in checkout before you send
  • Schedule sends so you're not sending outside 7 AM to 9 PM customer's local time
